About MoMetriX DataHub

A sales management and business analytics platform built specifically for small teams who need to track daily sales, manage products and stores, and transform raw data into actionable insights.

Our Story

MoMetriX DataHub was created to solve a common challenge faced by small businesses: managing sales data efficiently without the complexity and cost of enterprise solutions. We believe every business, regardless of size, deserves access to powerful analytics tools that help drive growth and inform decision-making.

Our platform combines intuitive sales tracking with comprehensive analytics, giving you the visibility you need to understand your business performance. From daily sales entry to detailed reports, MoMetriX DataHub streamlines your workflow so you can focus on what matters most—growing your business.

Our Mission

To empower small businesses with accessible, easy-to-use sales management and analytics tools that transform how they track, understand, and grow their operations.

Our Vision

To become the go-to platform for small teams seeking data-driven insights, making business intelligence accessible to everyone, not just large enterprises.

What We Offer

Daily sales tracking with real-time updates
Multi-store management for growing businesses
Product and inventory management
Comprehensive analytics and visualizations
CSV import/export for data portability
Team collaboration with role-based access
Stock adjustment tracking and reminders
Secure cloud-based platform
